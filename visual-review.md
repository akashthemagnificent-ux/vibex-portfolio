## Full-page Molten Metal redesign review

The first global-background pass made the material field present through the page, but desktop visibility was still too quiet. The luminance pass corrected that at mobile scale; however, it showed that the global material needs a stronger content-side veil because the supporting caption and “Begin the record” prompt become too low contrast against a bright metal pass. The final adjustment will keep the mobile material legible as a background while increasing local text contrast instead of reducing the shader itself.

The dark-gold refinement now leaves the hero headline, identity caption, supporting copy, and navigation legible at **390 × 844**, while the material field remains visibly active behind them. At desktop full-page scale, the background remains intentionally quieter behind text-heavy lower sections and visibly surfaces around the hero and Signal field; the continuous canvas is not replaced by static section imagery or hidden by opaque cards.

The final desktop review confirms a continuous, visible dark-gold WebGL material behind the first page with an unmistakable light-to-dark metal sweep across the composition. Hero text and navigation remain readable against the brighter material; content sections use translucent, not opaque, reading surfaces so the same live canvas persists as the page scrolls.
