# VIBEX — Reference-Led Experience Brief

The supplied self-interview video establishes the primary visual language for the redesign. The page should feel like a short, authored motion piece that happens to be interactive, not a dark portfolio with a 3D object added to it. Its pacing comes from a succession of full-screen scenes, each with one thought, one emotional beat, and a small number of precisely timed elements.

## What the previous version got wrong

The celestial-editorial build used acid-chartreuse controls, dense portfolio blocks, persistent navigation-like UI, and a fixed chat card. Those choices read as a premium creative-developer portfolio. They do not recreate the video’s deep-blue, dreamlike, self-interview atmosphere or its cinematic use of empty space.

## Visual Direction to Preserve

The redesign will use a **midnight blue to near-black void**, lit with soft electric-blue, lavender, and white bloom. The foreground will favor a high-contrast editorial serif for narration, paired with a small, widely tracked utility sans. Single lines must have room to breathe in the middle of the viewport. Background depth will come from drifting bokeh, blurred spheres, radial shutter-light trails, thin dotted paths, gentle grain, and occasional four-point glints—not from a single central monolith.

## Interaction Direction

The conversational idea stays, but it must become an **in-world self-interview mode** rather than a floating support widget. A single prompt such as “Ask Ash” opens a full-screen or large scene-aligned dialogue state. The response should appear as a cinematic transcript with a soft sound toggle; it should never resemble an app dashboard or generic chat bubble. The site will respect reduced-motion settings and will not use Ash’s face, voice, location, or private relationships.

## Explicit Design Bans

No acid-chartreuse signature color. No left-rail catalogue system. No generic rounded-card project grid. No persistent floating chat card. No technical-WebGL object as the visual protagonist. No crowded UI. The camera, text, and environment must carry the story.
