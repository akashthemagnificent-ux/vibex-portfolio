# COMMIT-SHEET — Vibex / Cobalt Proof Sheet

> **Register:** Auteur build. This is a single public personal-identity surface, not a forced scroll-film or a multi-route product.

## 1. Peak / Signature

The visitor meets a **Cobalt Proof Sheet** first: a large, physical-looking cobalt identity tile carrying a cut-out Vibex star. It behaves as a grounded object—tilting subtly with pointer movement and opening into the next page section—rather than as a logo, particle field, or generic decorative image.

## 2. Color

**Primary:** `oklch(0.57 0.22 258)` Signal Cobalt. **Tier:** committed; cobalt owns roughly forty percent of the first-screen surface and reappears only as an identity object, a link state, and a selected navigation state. It is neither lavender nor a blue-to-purple gradient: it is the lacquered, technical-blue ink of a private creative proof sheet. **Background target mean L:** `0.69`, a cool mineral paper (`oklch(0.94 0.007 250)`) under daylight; the page is lit because Ash is introducing himself, not hiding in a default “premium” black void.

## 3. Type

**Display:** Bricolage Grotesque, variable, used at high weight and close tracking for titles. **Text:** Manrope, humanist and highly readable for body copy and controls. The axis is expressive structural grotesque × quiet humanist sans. Inter is rejected because it would make the portfolio feel like a generic product landing page rather than an authored personal signal.

## 4. Grid Break

The cobalt proof sheet begins flush with the right viewport edge and crosses the hero’s baseline into the following section. Its diagonal fold carries the reader across the page boundary; the page never resolves into a centered, symmetrical hero.

## 5. Motion Budget

1. **Proof-sheet physicality:** pointer-driven tilt and a restrained fold-on-scroll transform on the singular hero object.
2. **Type assembly:** hero line fragments reveal through vertical clipping; section type uses one calm horizontal wipe rather than repeated fade-ups.
3. **Editorial rail transition:** active navigation marker travels along a fixed vertical rail as anchors become current.

All other UI feedback is instant or under 160ms. Motion uses only `transform` and `opacity`, and reduced motion becomes a still proof-sheet composition.

## 6. Reflex Check

**First-order portfolio reflex:** dark background, neon / violet glow, particle canvas, enormous wordmark, and a marquee. **Second-order reflex:** cream editorial page, oversized serif copy, rounded-card project grid, and a tiny mono technical label in every corner. **Chosen deviation:** a daylit, cobalt-lacquer identity artifact on mineral paper, with the story organized like a printed proof sheet rather than a template, terminal, or gallery wall.

## 7. Auteur House Tells Broken

1. **Near-black by default:** rejected. The main canvas is lit mineral paper, with black limited to ink and one compressed lower interlude.
2. **Mono service type in the corners:** rejected. There is no decorative status bar or corner metadata; utility text lives inside real controls and narrative captions.
3. **Wordmark as hero:** rejected. The physical proof sheet—not a giant `VIBEX` word—carries the peak.
4. **Glow as depth:** rejected. The proof sheet gets depth from hard lighting, controlled shadow, fold geometry, and material contrast rather than bloom.

## Content Boundary

This implementation remains a visual shell. It does not add undisclosed personal details, fabricated achievements, testimonials, or contact claims.
